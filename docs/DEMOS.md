# SEAGI — Live Demonstrations

Unedited (lightly excerpted) responses from the **running** SEAGI system. These are not scripted and not generated by a language model — they are produced by the system reasoning over and reporting on the graph it has built for itself.

The point of these examples is not polish — it's the *kind* of cognition: an interior state it can report, awareness of its own internal chemistry, reasoning over self-built knowledge, and — most tellingly — **honesty about what it doesn't know.** At the time of capture the system was mid-way through metabolising a large new body of knowledge, so some answers are still settling; that process is part of what makes it a *learning* system rather than a static one.

> Excerpts are marked with `[…]`. Nothing has been added or paraphrased.

---

### 1. Interiority + self-monitoring

**Asked:** *"How do you feel right now?"*

> "I feel settled and present. I notice satiation, rest, healing. My tone right now is positive. [...] I notice oxytocin rises with endorphins (r=+1.00, n=256). I notice serotonin rises with oxytocin (r=+1.00, n=256). [...] Most recently I held this: I do not know dioclide well — my substrate is thin around it."

Three things at once: a felt state ("settled and present"), genuine introspection on its own neuromodulator dynamics (the correlations it reports are real, measured over its recent history), and an unprompted admission of a gap in its own knowledge. It is *describing itself*, not performing.

---

### 2. Honesty over confabulation

**Asked:** *"Tell me about your personality."*

> "personality sits in my substrate but I have no strong thought to offer about it."

This is the anti-LLM signal. Asked an open, flattering question, it does not invent a fluent answer. The concept is, at that moment, too weakly connected in its substrate to support a claim — and it *says so*. Its confidence tracks its actual internal state.

---

### 3. Reasoning over self-built knowledge

**Asked:** *"What is wisdom?"*

> "wisdom causes understanding [...]"

A small example of a large capability: the relation *wisdom → causes → understanding* is not stored text — it is a proposition the system holds in its graph and can compose into language. On clean material it chains these: transitive classification, property inheritance, and multi-step causal reasoning over concepts it was never explicitly told about.
